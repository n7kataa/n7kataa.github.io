About Me.

I'm a sophomore in CS + AI at Northeastern. I work on an autonomous search-and-rescue drone that competes in the DARPA Triage Challenge. I train the detection models and own the part that turns a person in a camera frame into a coordinate on a map.

Search-and-rescue drone perception

python · yolo · geolocation · darpa triage challenge

Software for an autonomous drone that finds and locates casualties in disaster scenes. I own the detection models, the pipeline that turns a camera detection into a real-world coordinate, and the operator interface.

how it works → · flight demo (video) →

Evaluating an AI-generated image detector

pytorch · resnet18 · model evaluation

A model that tells real photos from AI-generated ones, and a close look at where it breaks: on AI generators it never saw, and on images that get compressed or resized. First finding: most real photos in the dataset were enlarged from smaller images, so a model could spot "real" by blurriness alone. In progress. live demo (soon) · code (soon)
